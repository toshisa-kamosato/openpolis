## 🏛️ What is Openpolis?

**Openpolis** is a platform for local self-governance and coordination designed to enable the birth and resilience of sovereign communities at a biological scale (Dunbar's scale, micronuclei < 200 people).

By combining **zero-friction analog tools** (clay QR code tokens, paper voting, and local exchange currency) with a **distributed, modular digital infrastructure** (local nodes, off-grid mesh protocols, and mutual credit ledgers), Openpolis restores political and economic power directly to the people.

---

## 🌟 Key Principles

* **Tactile Sovereignty (Zero Friction):** The transition from analog to digital happens naturally and progressively. A user's identity can reside on a physical clay token with a QR code and a PIN, without forcing anyone to manage complex cryptographic wallets.
* **Biological Scale (Dunbar's Limit):** Designed to operate at its best within compact groups based on trust and real mutual recognition (a physical Web of Trust), preventing Sybil attacks and authoritarian centralization.
* **Mutual Credit Economy (LUX):** A social monetary system based on real value and circulation (demurrage, 28-day clearing cycles, and credit caps) that discourages parasitic hoarding and funds community commons.
* **Polycentric Governance:** Direct democracy based on the strict principle of *1 person = 1 vote*, led by a local parliament and federable globally through fractal mesh networks.
* **Resilience & Antifragility:** A modular architecture capable of running offline (off-grid) via local networks (LoRaWAN/Wi-Fi) or synchronizing globally whenever connectivity becomes available.
